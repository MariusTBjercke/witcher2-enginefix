# Saves on GOG (0.10.0, phase 2), 2026-11-04

GOG 3.5.0.26g, vanilla scripts plus A Better UI, native Direct3D 9 (no DXVK), 4K. The shipped ini
with `skip_intro=1` and, for this run only, `save_trace=1`, `savelist_trace=1` and `fast_list=2`
(odd list builds fast, even ones full). The working build: the GOG save port
(`docs/fixes/save-previews.md` and `save-bloat.md`, "GOG"), on top of `e199dc0`. 118 saves in
`Documents\Witcher 2\gamesaves` at the start.

The owner loaded a save in Flotsam and made three manual saves and one quicksave. They opened the
Load menu several times and went through a few house doors. Then they quit through the pause
menu. Owner: "the pictures looked fine". The doors were abrupt and showed the loading spinner,
because `door_light_blend` and the hidden spinner were not ported to GOG in this build. No dump.

The fix noted each of these previews and added nothing: there is no `thumbnail: wrote` line, and
every BMP is 921 656 bytes, the size of a live capture. On GOG the race is lost only some of the
time: 14 of the older local saves have no preview. The late write is covered offline by
`tools/buildprobe` ("thumbnail (GOG)"); in game it is still to be seen.

The Load menu opens every preview from disk on each opening (`access=80000000 disp=3`, a burst of
BMP reads after each list build). No image registry is involved (`previews held=0` throughout).
That confirms the static finding that GOG's list keeps no previews.

The `.sav` part drops from ~0.85 s to ~0.05 s. Without Steam's network read, GOG's fast list is
cheaper than Steam's (~0.24 s for the `.sav` part there).
