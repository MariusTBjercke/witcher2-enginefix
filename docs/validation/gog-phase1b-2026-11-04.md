Owner's test after porting `VA_GAME`, `VA_FACTS_TIME`, `VA_RAY_*`, `VA_STARTVIDEO_*`, the `WF_*`
window sites with `WF_CONFIG`, and `WK_*` (`docs/fixes/gog-build.md`, "Ported addresses"). GOG
3.5.0.26g with the shipped ini plus `skip_intro=1`, vanilla scripts and A Better UI; the shared
User.ini had Borderless on, Full screen off and the Draw distance row at extreme. Then a short
Steam start with the same DLL.

* Both guards installed at their `k_gog` addresses plus the delta `0x00930000` (`00E08740`,
  `0050B0A9`).
* Skip intro: GOG's startup list has two entries (Steam's one); both were stopped and the main
  menu reached, through the GOG stubs that pass the player in ECX.
* The Options rows reach the DLL through GOG's `__thiscall` writer hook: Display mode switched
  live (Borderless off, Full screen on, Full screen off, Borderless on), and the Draw distance row
  changed the preset several times. No crash, no dump.
* The keyboard became non-exclusive behind the hooked window procedure (Windows keys).
* The log ends at the CRT's shutdown filter change again, without `unloaded` (see the open item
  in `docs/fixes/gog-build.md`). Nothing in the log shows the pause menu's Quit with an area
  loaded, so the quit guard's catch is not seen on GOG yet; its count is only written at detach.
